SINTEX-F2 simulations and linear models

These simulations and models underpin my research on the Southern Hemisphere circumpolar wavenumber-4 pattern. With the SINTEX-F2 coupled model, we showed that SST variability in the southwestern subtropical Pacific triggers the atmospheric variability that generates the pattern during austral summer, while tropical SST variability has a limited effect (Senapati et al., 2024, JGR-Oceans).

In the sensitivity experiments, SST in selected regions is kept at its climatology, removing its variability there, to isolate where the pattern originates.

Linear models

Linear barotropic model

Used to examine Rossby wave propagation and the atmospheric response to idealised forcing.

Linear response model

A semi-empirical linear step-response model (following Deb et al., 2020), used to estimate the Southern Hemisphere extratropical response to a step-like change in convection over the Rossby wave source region in the western subtropical Pacific (Senapati et al., 2022, Climate Dynamics).
